Category card imagery
=====================

honeymoon.jpg      Frame 100 of /public/frames-2 (hero clip 2).
                   ffmpeg crop=990:660:145:0, scaled to 900x600.
international.jpg  Frame 100 of /public/frames-4 (hero clip 4).
                   ffmpeg crop=880:587:400:20, scaled to 900x600.
luxury.jpg         Frame 80 of /public/coastal-banner.
                   ffmpeg crop=840:560:340:0, scaled to 900x600.

All three are cropped so that the generator watermark baked into the
bottom-right of the source frames falls outside the frame. Re-crop from the
same sources if these ever need regenerating; do not scale these files up.

These are generated scenes, not photographs of identifiable places, so their
alt text in QuickTravelCategories.tsx describes what is shown rather than
naming a destination. The other six category cards use real destination
photography from /public/destinations and /public/package-gallery — see the
CREDITS.txt in each of those folders for sourcing.

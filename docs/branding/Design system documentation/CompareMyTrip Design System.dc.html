01 — Foundation

Brand foundation

CompareMyTrip sells travel packages, not flights. The product journey is Discover → Explore → Compare → Package detail → Enquire. Premium but accessible. Modern but not futuristic. Friendly but not childish. Bold but not aggressive. Minimal but not empty.

DO
  • Feel optimistic and forward-moving
  • Keep interfaces clear and scannable above all
  • Use bold contrast purposefully — one yellow action
  • Give content room to breathe
  • Reuse established patterns before inventing
DO NOT
  • Use gradients as page backgrounds
  • Make the interface futuristic — no neon, no heavy glass
  • Overuse yellow (hard cap: 10% of any viewport)
  • Add decoration that carries no meaning
  • Ship a shrunken desktop layout as "mobile"

Measure

Display headlines cap at ~20ch. Body Large runs 60–72ch (max 680px), Body Regular 60–75ch (max 640px). Card body truncates at 3 lines desktop, 4 mobile.

Two-tone headline

The line always breaks before the yellow segment. On light use #A37600 at ≥32px; on dark use #FFCB19. Never a bright yellow below 32px on white — and never orange simply because it contrasts more.

Legibility floor

11px absolute minimum, 14px body minimum, 16px minimum on inputs. Caption and Label clamp at 11px on tablet and mobile.

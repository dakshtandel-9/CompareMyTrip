# Color destination stamp assets

The ten `public/visaLogo/*-color.webp` files are AI-colored adaptations of the repository’s original monochrome `visa1.png` through `visa10.png`, created with the built-in imagegen tool. Matching color originals were not located. These are decorative travel illustrations, not official postage or immigration stamps.

Each output is 512 × 512 WebP at quality 90. The original monochrome inputs remain available as source references. `src/app/add-on/AddOnBody.tsx` displays the color files with new URLs to avoid cached monochrome versions.

## Prompts

### Japan

Edit the original Japan destination souvenir stamp into a full-color illustrated travel stamp. Preserve its square canvas, rounded rectangular double border, composition, Mount Fuji, cherry blossoms, sun, aircraft, postal waves and exact lettering: JAPAN / TRADITION MEETS TOMORROW. Use vivid pink blossoms, red sun, blue Mount Fuji with white snow, turquoise water, green leaves, navy/crimson border and navy typography. Keep crisp engraved illustration texture with full-color scenery, pure white exterior, complete uncropped stamp, no shadows or additional lettering.
